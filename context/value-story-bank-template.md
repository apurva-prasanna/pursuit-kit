# value-story-bank.md — Proven Stories and Plays
*Built through use over time. Added to via plays command or manually after meetings.*

---

## How to Use This File

Each entry is a proven story or play that has worked in a real client
conversation. Add new entries after meetings where something landed well.
Over time this becomes your most valuable asset — institutional knowledge
that survives rep turnover and makes every future pursuit stronger.

Write stories at a level of abstraction that doesn't identify clients
e.g. "a major FMCG brand in Southeast Asia" not the client name.

---

## Story Template

Use this structure for each entry:

### [Story Title]
- **Pain it addresses**: What client problem does this story speak to?
- **Buyer it works with**: Who in the room does this land best with?
  (e.g. CMO, CFO, Head of Digital, agency lead)
- **When to deploy**: What signal or moment in the conversation
  triggers this story?
- **The story**: 3-5 sentences. What was the situation, what did
  you do, what was the result?
- **The number**: What's the specific metric or outcome that makes
  this credible?
- **Strength rating**: How proven is this? (Tested and landed /
  Used once / Hypothetical)
